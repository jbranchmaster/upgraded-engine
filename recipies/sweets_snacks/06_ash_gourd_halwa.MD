
# Ash Gourd Halwa

Ash gourd halwa is a sweet made from ash gourd. Ash gourd is made up of water, expect **25%** usable pulp for halwa.

# Core Ingredients
- Ash gourd - at least 2 kg. Wash it, cut it into big slices grate.  
- Sugar `1.5 glass`
- Milk   `1.5 glasses`. Boil the milk and keep it aside.
- Cashew `8-10`. Cut then into small pieces. 
- Elaichi `4 to 6`
- Ghee `1-2 tbsp`
