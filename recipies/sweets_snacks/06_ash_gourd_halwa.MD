
# Ash Gourd Halwa

Ash gourd halwa is a sweet made from ash gourd. Ash gourd is made up of water, expect **25%** usable pulp for halwa.

# Core Ingredients
- This recipe is for a **500gm** ash gourd pulp without water.
- Ash gourd - at least 2 kg. Wash it, cut it into big slices, then peel the skin and then grate it.  
- Sugar
- Saffron `~8-10` strands
- Cashew `8-10`. Cut one half of cashew into 4 pieces.
- Elaichi `4 to 6`
- Ghee `1-2 tbsp`.


# Preparation
- Take grated ash gourd and drain its water using cloth. Do not throw the water.
- Weigh the ash gourd without water and take equal amounts of sugar.
- Take ash gourd, its water and sugar into a vessel and bring it to a boil.
- Mix well and cook on medium flame until water almost evaporates. Takes about 1 hour for 500gm  water less ash gourd.
- At this point, add `elachi powder`, `ghee`, `cashews` and  `saffron strands`.
- Let it cool and store it in a air tight container.
